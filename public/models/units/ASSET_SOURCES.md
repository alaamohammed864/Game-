# 3D unit model sources

The GLB files in this directory are bundled locally so the globe does not depend on third-party model hosting at runtime.

| File | Source | License | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `f15-fighter.glb` | [Innerscene F-15 Eagle](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/f-15-eagle-fighter-jet-e1d11142) | CC0 1.0 | Original, textured 48k-triangle model. |
| `main-battle-tank.glb` | [3DAssets.dev Main Battle Tank](https://3dassets.dev/packs/armoured-column-and-field-camp) | CC0 1.0 | Generic tank model from the listed pack; contributor reports AI generation. |
| `ship-large.glb` and `Textures/colormap.png` | [Kenney Watercraft Kit: Ship Large](https://kenney.nl/assets/watercraft-kit) | CC0 1.0 | Generic watercraft model and its required texture, distributed from Flockbay's Kenney mirror. |

The models are normalized for globe-scale visualization and are not rendered at their real-world size. The CC0 sources allow modification and redistribution; attribution is not required.
